# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1 — summary)

Our Pledge
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

Community Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable Behaviour
Harassment, insults, hate speech, discrimination, or threats will not be tolerated.

Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at conduct@zururateamsafaris.example (replace with a real contact). Reports will be reviewed and responded to promptly and fairly.

Scope
This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
